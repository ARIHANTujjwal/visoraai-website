Accessibility

Access through sound, not more interface.

VisoraAI is built around users who may not be able to depend on the screen. The product direction prioritizes spoken reading and practical camera guidance.

Spoken output first

The main output is audio. The point of the system is to convert printed information into spoken language.

Useful camera guidance

Feedback such as hold steady, move closer, or reduce glare helps the user improve capture quality without needing to visually inspect the frame.

Readable web defaults

The site uses comfortable spacing, readable text, visible keyboard focus, and optional display settings for contrast, text size, and motion.
