Research

Testing OCR where cameras usually fail.

VisoraAI is a prototype for studying how camera quality, motion, and image processing affect text recognition and spoken reading output.

Challenge

Motion blur

Handheld camera movement can smear characters before OCR sees them. Burst capture and frame selection help reduce dependence on one unstable image.

Challenge

Glare and lighting

Printed pages often reflect light unevenly. Glare checks and guidance can help the user adjust the page before the system attempts recognition.

Evaluation

Character-level accuracy

OCR quality can be measured by comparing recognized characters against ground truth text. Character Error Rate gives a more precise view than simply asking whether a sentence “looks right.”

Future direction

Custom OCR model

The project also includes experimentation around a CRNN-style OCR model, synthetic training data, character sets, and CTC decoding.
