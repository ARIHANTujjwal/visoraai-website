Roadmap

Future directions for VisoraAI.

The current prototype focuses on camera-based printed text reading. Future versions could make the system more portable, local, and flexible.

01

Mobile reading

Move the prototype toward a phone-based reading assistant for everyday use.

02

Offline support

Improve local OCR and text-to-speech so the system can work with less internet dependence.

03

Wearable camera support

Explore a hands-free reading setup using a wearable or mounted camera.

04

Multilingual recognition

Expand beyond English text recognition as the system becomes more capable.
