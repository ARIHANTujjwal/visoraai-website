System map

Camera input becomes spoken output through five system layers.

The architecture is designed to reduce uncertainty before the final text is read aloud. Click a layer for deeper analysis.

Camera capture Detection Enhancement OCR Speech
01

Capture

The camera provides the live frame. The system has to work with imperfect angle, hand movement, and changing lighting.

02

Locate

Page and text-region detection reduce the amount of background content sent into OCR.

03

Enhance

Contrast, blur, glare, and sharpness checks improve text quality before recognition.

04

Recognize

The OCR layer extracts text and can use fallback logic when confidence is low.

05

Speak

Text-to-speech turns the recognized text into the actual accessible output.
